Giovanni Zullo

Nuclear fuel modelling · multi-physics FEM simulation · open-source code development
Research assistant (Assegno di Ricerca) — Milan, Italy

Postdoctoral researcher in nuclear engineering working on the multi-scale, multi-physics modelling and simulation of nuclear fuel behaviour. My research bridges two complementary directions: finite-element, multi-physics simulation of the coupled thermo-mechanical and damage behaviour of nuclear fuel — as main developer and maintainer of the open-source Z3ST framework — and physics-based modelling of gaseous and volatile fission-product behaviour, through the open-source SCIANTIX code, coupled to fuel performance codes such as TRANSURANUS, OFFBEAT and FRAPCON/FRAPTRAN. My approach centres on model development, numerical simulation, and rigorous verification, validation and uncertainty quantification (VVUQ) for fuel safety analysis under nominal, off-normal and accidental conditions, across Gen-III light water reactors and Gen-IV fast reactor systems.
